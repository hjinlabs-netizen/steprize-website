HOW IT WORKS

Steprize leverages advanced Android sensor architecture to verify your physical movement in real-time. By utilizing a sophisticated fusion of the device's high-precision STEP_DETECTOR and STEP_COUNTER sensors, the app captures every stride with minimal battery impact.

To ensure the authenticity of your activity, our Fused Location Provider intelligently cross-references movement with GPS data. This multi-layered validation prevents simulated activity, ensuring that every COIN you earn represents genuine physical effort.

All data is securely synchronized with our backend via encrypted channels. As you walk, the app calculates your earnings based on your active sneakers and credits your balance instantly, creating a seamless bridge between your fitness goals and tangible rewards.
